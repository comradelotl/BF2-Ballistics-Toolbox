⑦ Deviation

Get a minDev from a given range and target size, or the hit chance from a given minDev.

Four zones hold all shots: red (25%), yellow (50 %), green (75%), blue (99%). The ring represents the average distance of a shot from the aim point.

RMS MOA:
Typical distance a shot lands from the aimpoint centre. minDev 1.0 gives about 46 RMS MOA (measured on 250 shots).
Extreme spread MOA (ES):
Largest distance between any two shots of a group. The values shown are the mean for a 5-shot group, about 2.17 × RMS, so minDev 1.0 gives about 100 MOA. Larger groups spread wider.

minDev = 0.01 or smaller will collapse to zero deviation.

Hit chance:
Probability that a single shot lands on the target (a square) when aimed at its centre. The scatter is round, centred and bell-curve shaped (checked on 250 measured shots). The plausible range assumes the scatter constant is uncertain by ±10% (95% interval from those shots).
