minDev 1.0 test

Question: how big is the spread of minDev set to 1.0?

Setup: 
Test gun: Phallanx 
Screen resolution 1920x1080
Camera FOV 0.17453 = 10°, or 600 MOA, or 600/1080 = 0.555 MOA per pixel.

Method: 5-shot groups, measuring the pixel distance of the two most distant shots in MOA.

Results: average of 104.4204819 MOA 
and a standard deviation of ~30 MOA

Conclusion 0.01 is ~1 MOA ±0.3 SD

I doubt that the devs parametrized minDev as MOA. 
Alternative interpretations that fit into the uncertainty range would include 
1.75 degree, which is 105 MOA or
0.03 radians, which is 103.13 MOA.
